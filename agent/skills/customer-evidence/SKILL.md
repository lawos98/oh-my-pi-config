# Customer Evidence

1. Establish the decision/question and authorized dataset. Confirm consent/use restrictions and retention requirements from the task; if unavailable, identify that boundary before using sensitive material. Do not import CRM records or contact people.
2. Work from deidentified records. Retain only needed fields; replace direct identifiers with stable local evidence IDs. Do not copy unnecessary personal data into quotes, logs, reports, or shared artifacts. Describe redactions without exposing originals.
3. Inventory source, collection dates/channel, recruitment method, cohort, unique participants, record count, and missing data. Distinguish repeated comments from independent people. Document exclusions and duplicates so denominators are reproducible.
4. Code themes against the question, preserving contradictory and negative cases. Separate exact quotations, paraphrases, observations, and interpretations. Cite each quotation to an authorized source pointer and evidence ID; do not invent, splice, or silently rewrite quotes. Mark redactions and excerpts.
5. For counts or percentages, state numerator, denominator, unit, and whether multiple themes per respondent were allowed. Calculate survey metrics only when question wording, scale, eligible responses, and missing-response handling support that metric; otherwise report the gap. Do not infer causal effects from descriptive feedback.
6. Assess selection, recall, channel, survivorship, and response biases; distinguish sampled participants from the intended population. Frequency is not automatically importance. Label hypotheses and confidence limits rather than claiming market validation.
7. Return a compact evidence table (claim/theme, supporting IDs/quotes, counterevidence, sample limits), decision implications, unanswered questions, and the evidence needed to distinguish alternatives. Ensure every factual conclusion has a traceable source or is explicitly labeled an inference.

No outreach, account access, automated imports, transactions, manufactured testimonials, unsupported effect claims, or legal/consent clearance by assumption. This is passive synthesis, not authority to collect or publish customer information.
