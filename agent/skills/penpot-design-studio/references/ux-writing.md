# UX writing

Read behavior and status definitions before editing labels. Use consistent nouns and verb-led actions. Separate what happened, what remains uncertain and what the person can do next. Practical labels must remain comprehensible without fantasy lore.

Keep the System impersonal where the brief requires it; other senders have their own identity. Explain pending, failed and unknown outcomes differently. Never turn "upload pending" into confirmed progress, a display-only reward into a claim action, or a proposed sharing rule into a promise.

Draft the normal, empty, loading, validation, failure and recovery copy relevant to the task. Preserve units, consequence warnings and return paths. Prefer a concrete next action to motivational filler. Keep fictional data visibly fictional and numeric balance provisional.

Review real text lengths on the native boards. Check enlarged text and a Polish probe when relevant; a translated probe does not approve launch localization. Use consistent fixture revisions across variants. Deliver copy changes with source IDs and before/after meaning checks, not just a detached tone guide.
